KAZ updates are live

October 4, 2026

This is the first update sent through kaz-update. Instead of a wall of screenshots, an update is written once, published as a single page, and sent as one iMessage bubble. Tap the link and the whole thing is here.

Why a page and not a screenshot

  • iOS Messages draws a preview card from the page's Open Graph tags, so the

bubble says what the update is before it is opened.

  • The update can be as long as it needs to be without cluttering a thread.
  • It survives scrolling and can be reopened, shared, or read on a laptop.

How it works

The page is unlisted: every page is noindex, slugs carry a random suffix, and robots.txt disallows the whole site.
